GTA V Strong comeback in the Gulf

Date: 2025–2026

News: Official release in Saudi Arabia and the UAE

Details:

                    Rockstar Games’ popular title Grand Theft Auto V (GTA V) has now officially launched in Saudi Arabia and UAE after the delay of almost 12 years. The game which was released on July 17, 2025 marks a turning point in media regulation within the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. The game which was originally launched on 2013 was banned in the Gulf region due to its mature themes including drug use, sexual content and violence. 
                    However, from July 17, 2025 the game became officially available on PlayStation, Xbox and PC. The release of the game was made possible due to the 21+ age rating — a first-of-its-kind classification in the region.
