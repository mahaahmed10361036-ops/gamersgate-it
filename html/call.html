How to Play Call of Duty?

Because Call of Duty (CoD) is a massive franchise with many titles (like Modern Warfare, Black Ops, and Warzone), the gameplay focuses on fast-paced, first-person shooter (FPS) mechanics. 🎮 Basic Controls (PC) WASD: Move your character. Shift (Hold): Sprint (Double-tap for "Tactical Sprint" in newer games). Spacebar: Jump / Mantle (climb over obstacles). C / Ctrl: Crouch / Prone (lay down). Left-Click: Fire weapon. Right-Click (Hold): Aim Down Sights (ADS) for better accuracy. R: Reload your weapon. G / Mouse 4: Throw Lethal Equipment (Grenades, Semtex). E / Mouse 5: Use Tactical Equipment (Flashbangs, Stun Grenades). V: Melee attack (hit with your gun or knife). 🛡️ Core Gameplay Mechanics Loadouts: Before a match, you customize your "Class." This includes a primary weapon (Assault Rifle, Sniper), a secondary weapon (Pistol, Launcher), and Perks (passive abilities like "Ghost" to stay off radar or "Scavenger" to pick up ammo). Health Regeneration: In most CoD games, your health regenerates automatically if you stop taking damage for a few seconds. In Warzone, you must manually apply Armor Plates. Killstreaks / Scorestreaks: If you get several kills in a row without dying, you unlock powerful rewards like a UAV (shows enemies on the map), an Airstrike, or a Chopper Gunner. The Mini-Map: Always keep an eye on the top-left corner. Red dots appear when enemies fire unsuppressed weapons, giving away their position. 🔥 Pro Tips for Multiplayer Don't "Sprint" Around Corners: If you are sprinting and see an enemy, it takes longer to raise your gun and shoot. Walk or "ADS" when turning corners where enemies might be. Mount Your Weapon: In newer titles, you can "Mount" your gun on walls or crates. This significantly reduces recoil and makes you a smaller target. Play the Objective: In modes like Domination or Hardpoint, kills are good, but capturing the zones is what actually wins the game and gives you the most XP. Sound Whore (Use Headphones): Listening for enemy footsteps is one of the biggest advantages you can have. It allows you to know exactly where someone is coming from before you see them.

Game Modes

  • Single Player Modes Grand Prix: The classic "Campaign" mode where you race through a series of tracks to win a trophy.
    Time Trials: A solo mode where the goal is to beat your own best time or a "ghost" of another player's run.
    Practice/Training: A sandbox mode where players can test controls and learn the map without any pressure.
  • Multiplayer Modes Versus (VS): A standard race or match where players compete directly against each other to reach the finish line or get the most points.
    Battle Mode: Instead of racing, players use items to pop each other's balloons or drain their health in a closed arena.
    Co-op: Players work together on the same team to achieve a goal, like defeating a boss or surviving waves of enemies.
