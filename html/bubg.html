How to Play Pupg?

Playing PUBG (PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds) is all about survival, strategy, and a bit of luck. The core goal is to be the last person or team standing among 100 players. Here is a beginner’s guide to mastering the basics: 1. The Drop (Starting the Match) The game begins in a cargo plane flying over a massive map. Pick a Spot: Mark a location on your map. "Hot drops" (like Pochinki or Military Base) have great loot but are crowded with enemies. Remote clusters of houses are safer for beginners. Parachuting: Dive straight down to reach maximum speed (234 km/h) if you want to land first. Landing quickly is vital so you can find a weapon before anyone else. 2. Looting (Gearing Up) Once you hit the ground, your priority is finding equipment. You start with nothing but your clothes. Weapons: Look for a versatile primary weapon like an M416 or AKM (Assault Rifles). A Sniper Rifle or DMR is great for long-range engagements. Armor: Find a Helmet and a Vest. These come in three levels (Level 1 to Level 3). A Level 3 helmet is the only thing that can save you from a sniper's headshot. Medical Supplies: Collect First Aid Kits, Bandages, and "Boosters" (Painkillers and Energy Drinks). Boosters heal you over time and make you run faster. 3. The "Circle" (The Blue Zone) The map is constantly shrinking to force players together. Safe Zone (White Circle): This is where you need to be. Blue Zone: If you are outside the white circle when the timer ends, the blue "electric" wall will close in. Being caught in it drains your health. The Red Zone: Occasionally, a red circle appears on the map. This area is about to be bombed—get inside a building immediately! 4. Combat Tips Use Cover: Never run in a straight line across an open field. Move from tree to tree or rock to rock. Listen Closely: Sound is your best friend. Use headphones to hear enemy footsteps or distant gunfire. This tells you exactly where the danger is. Patience Wins: You don't have to kill everyone to win. Sometimes, staying hidden and letting other players kill each other is the best way to reach the "Top 10." 5. Basic Controls (PC Version) WASD: Movement F: Interact (open doors, pick up loot) Right-Click: Aim down sights (ADS) Left-Click: Shoot C: Crouch / Z: Prone (lie down) Spacebar: Jump/Vault over obstacles M: Map If you manage to survive until the very end, you’ll see the famous screen: "Winner Winner Chicken Dinner!"

Game Modes

  • Classic Mode (Battle Royale) Solo: You against 99 other players. High pressure, no one to revive you.
    Duo: You and one partner. Coordination is key.
    Squad: A team of 4 players. This is the most popular mode, involving strategy, roles (like sniper or medic), and team revives.
  • Arcade Modes Team Deathmatch (TDM): Usually 8v8. You spawn with a specific weapon loadout in a small arena. The first team to reach the kill limit wins.
    War Mode: A mix of Battle Royale and Deathmatch. You parachute in with a weapon and respawn shortly after dying.
    Quick Match: A mini version of Classic mode that lasts about 8 minutes, usually set in a very small portion of a map.
